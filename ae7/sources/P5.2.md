CONTEXT NOTES:
Austria-Hungary occupied Bosnia-Herzegovina under the 1878 treaty; it announced annexation on October 6–7, 1908, a day or two after Bulgaria declared independence (October 5). The Ottoman Empire accepted compensation in February 1909; Russia backed down in March 1909.
