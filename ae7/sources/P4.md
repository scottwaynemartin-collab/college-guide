IMAGE NOTES:
The caption describes what is visible: the old pilot in a peaked cap and double-breasted coat stepping down a ladder on the ship's side; a young man with an upturned moustache, jeweled crown and uniform with shoulder boards leaning on the rail with folded arms; the title "Dropping the Pilot"; the top line "Punch, or the London Charivari.—March 29, 1890." The caption does not name Bismarck or William II (E7-P10 asks students to identify the event). The caption adds one sentence explaining what a ship's pilot was.

CAPTION NOTE (Nov 9, 2026): the student caption first said the pilot "steps down a rope ladder"; the picture shows a ladder with wooden steps and a rope handrail, so the caption now says "steps down a ladder hung over the side."

CONTEXT NOTES:
Bismarck submitted his resignation on March 18, 1890, at William II's demand; it was accepted on March 20. The Reinsurance Treaty lapsed in June 1890. Not used: Tenniel's other Punch cartoons.

DESCRIPTION NOTE (Nov 9, 2026, v2026-11-09c): a reviewer suggested the object at the foot of the ladder is a railed platform, not a boat. Re-viewed raw/commons/tenniel_960.jpg (bottom third): it is the curved, planked side of a small open boat with two thole pins (oar pegs) on its gunwale, so it stays a boat. The screen-reader description no longer says the boat "waits" (an interpretation); it now says "At the foot of the ladder, the curved side of a small rowboat is visible." The student caption is unchanged.
