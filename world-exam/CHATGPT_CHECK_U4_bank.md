**U4-P01** [Topic 4.2 · LO C · Skill 3.A]  The answer "in search of Christians and of spices" in the first paragraph best reflects which motives for Portuguese exploration?
- (A) Religious and commercial goals: finding Christian allies and reaching the spice trade
- (B) A wish to escape religious persecution at home and settle in new lands
- (C) A plan to conquer and colonize the interior of the Indian subcontinent
- (D) Scientific curiosity about the plants, animals, and peoples of the Southern Hemisphere
KEY: A. Explanation: "Christians" points to religion: the Portuguese hoped to find Christian allies and spread their faith. "Spices" points to money: they wanted to buy spices at the source instead of through Muslim and Venetian middlemen.
  - why not B: The Portuguese were not fleeing persecution; they were sent by their king.
  - why not C: Nothing here mentions conquering inland India; the Portuguese wanted ports and trade.
  - why not D: The crew says they came for Christians and spices, not to study nature.
  - hint: Split the phrase in two. What does "Christians" tell you? What does "spices" tell you?
