CONTEXT NOTES:
The author is Edward Baines Jr. (1800-1890), not his father Edward Baines (1774-1848), who owned the Leeds Mercury and also wrote histories. The son became a junior editor of the paper about 1820, a partner in 1827, and sole editor in 1834, when his father was elected to Parliament; he became proprietor only after his father's death in 1848 (English Wikipedia, 'Edward Baines (1800-1890)', checked Nov. 8, 2026, agreeing with the independent check report). In 1835 he was therefore the paper's editor. Attribution and credit changed in v2026-11-09b from 'Edward Baines, newspaper owner' to 'Edward Baines Jr., editor of the Leeds Mercury'.
