Vietnam, Law on Intellectual Property (2005, as amended), art. 15(2): works not protected by copyright include "legal documents, administrative documents and other documents in the judicial domain and official translations of such documents." The note is a formal diplomatic note of the DRV Ministry of Foreign Affairs to four governments; its English text was prepared by the DRV itself (Ho Chi Minh handed copies to a U.S. official), so it is an official version, not a private translation. Vietnam became eligible for U.S. restoration only on joining the Berne Convention (2004); the exclusion of state documents in Vietnamese law at that date (the 1995 Civil Code's copyright chapter; not separately checked here) means the note should not have been restored under 17 U.S.C. § 104A. The Pentagon Papers (a U.S. government publication) are only the printed source of the text; printing there is not itself the basis.
