(Revision of Nov 8: the caravan and BOAC sentences were restored, as the independent check recommended, so the Indian government's own request and its lack of planes are shown.) Balance note (revised Nov 8 after the ChatGPT review): an editor's footnote says that armed groups and individuals from Hindu, Muslim, and Sikh communities attacked civilians from other communities, that members of all three communities also fled, and that the telegram reports one attack, not all Partition violence (see also FRUS 1947 III, doc. 113, where the Indian ambassador blames Sikh and RSS instigators for the Delhi violence).
