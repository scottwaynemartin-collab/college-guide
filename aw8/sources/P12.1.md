2. Para 2: "Prime Minister through Bajpai … immediate peril." Cut (…): "They cannot be sent by caravan … GOI has available sixteen BOAC Dakotas only a few of which in condition to fly. Attack on caravan has not been reported Delhi." Then "When news gets out … beyond anything yet seen." Cut (…): the rest (Nehru's government may fall; request for MacArthur's planes).
Balance note: a footnote tells students that Hindus, Sikhs, and Muslims in the Punjab all attacked members of other communities in 1947 (see also FRUS 1947 III, doc. 113, where the Indian ambassador blames Sikh and RSS instigators for the Delhi violence).
