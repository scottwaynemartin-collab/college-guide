What the image shows (checked by viewing the 960-px file): a low-angle aerial view of bare, rutted ground among royal palms; title box at upper left "MRBM LAUNCH SITE 2 / SAN CRISTOBAL / 1 NOVEMBER 1962"; white leader lines to labels "MISSILE-READY TENT" (one standing tent), "FORMER LAUNCH POSITIONS" (two bare patches), "FUEL TRAILERS" (a group of tanker trucks), and "FORMER LOCATION OF MISSILE-READY TENTS." The photo documents the site being dismantled after Khrushchev's October 28 agreement.
Caption choices: the caption transcribes the title box and labels exactly and describes the ground; it does not say "Cuban Missile Crisis," "Soviet," or "nuclear" (tested by U8-P19 and P21). The attribution says "U.S. military reconnaissance photograph" because Commons classes it as U.S. Air Force while low-level flights over Cuba in November 1962 were flown by both Air Force and Navy units. Glossary: reconnaissance, MRBM (expanded only), missile-ready tent.
