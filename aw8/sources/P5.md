Author + date: Ambassador John Leighton Stuart (Nanjing) to the Secretary of State, despatch no. 267, June 9, 1948, enclosing texts of speeches broadcast by the Chinese Communists' North Shensi Radio in late April and early May 1948, with the embassy's "brief summary" of them. The bank prints the embassy's summary (not the Chinese texts, which FRUS does not print): (1) Ren Bishi (Jen Pi-shih), remarks of January 12, 1948, to the Northwest Liberated Army's Front Committee; (3) Mao Zedong, address of April 1, 1948, to cadres of the Shansi-Suiyuan Liberated Area.
