Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China, art. 5(1) (laws and regulations of State organs "and their official translations" are not protected); the Foreign Languages Press was the PRC government's official publisher of translations. Not protected in its source country on the URAA date, so not restored in the U.S. under 17 U.S.C. § 104A.
This source replaces the U.S. embassy's 1948 summary of speeches by Ren Bishi and Mao (FRUS 1948, vol. VII, doc. 227), so the Chinese government's own text is used instead of a U.S. summary. The law's ban on "indiscriminate arrest, beating or killing" is printed, and the explanation for U8-P14 tells students that land reform was in fact often violent.
