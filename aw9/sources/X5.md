Corrections and editorial changes:
- Source 1: OCR "Thes2 developments" -> "These developments". The paragraph beginning "On one matter there must be no doubt" was spoken in Afrikaans; the English is FBIS's translation (the attribution says so).
- Source 2: FBIS's notes on crowd reactions ("[crowd cheers]", "[crowd shouts: /Viva!/]", "[as heard]") are left out without ellipses; they are not Mandela's words.
- Source 2: the TV transcript's garbled sentence "The fracas which necessitated the armed struggle still exists today" is cut (SAPA's report of the same speech, printed on the same FBIS page, has "The factors which necessitated the armed struggle still exist today").
- Source 2: "a common voters role" (FBIS's transcription of the spoken word) is printed "a common voters' roll".
- Source 2: the brackets "[African National Congress]" and "[Spear of the Nation]" are FBIS's own.
- Source 2 (2026-11-08b): "We have no option but to continue [the armed struggle]." The bracket supplies the object of "continue", which the cut of the garbled FBIS sentence had removed.
- Glossary (2026-11-08b): the ANC is glossed as "a political organization founded in 1912 to win rights for Black South Africans; the government banned it in 1960" (the earlier gloss called it "the oldest Black political organization," which is open to dispute).
