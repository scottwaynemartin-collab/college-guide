Documents: (1) Telegram, Nicholas II to Wilhelm II, Peterhof Palace, 29 July 1914, 1 p.m. (German White Book, Exhibit 21). (2) Telegram, Wilhelm II to Nicholas II, 30 July 1914, 1 a.m. (Exhibit 23). The German government published the exchange in its White Book ("Das deutsche Weißbuch"), August 1914.
Edition: Collected Diplomatic Documents Relating to the Outbreak of the European War (London: HMSO, 1915), "The German White Book," pp. 431–432.
URL curled: https://archive.org/download/cu31924026425144/cu31924026425144_djvu.txt -> raw/cdd1915_cu31924026425144.txt (lines 30395–30480)
PD note: published 1915 (British government); public domain in the US.
CUTS: none inside either telegram. Source 1 omits the heading and signature "(Signed) Nicolas."; Source 2 omits the signature "(Signed) Wilhelm."
OCR notes: "Em'opean" -> "European"; "1 shall" -> "I shall"; quotation mark before "a weak country" in the OCR is a scan artifact and is dropped.
FOOTNOTE: "Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia on July 28, 1914" (explains "a weak country").
CONTEXT: Austria-Hungary declared war 28 July; Russian general mobilization ordered 30 July (announced 31 July); Germany declared war on Russia 1 Aug and France 3 Aug; invaded Belgium 4 Aug; Britain declared war 4 Aug.
