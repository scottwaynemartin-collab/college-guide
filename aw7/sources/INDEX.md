# INDEX: AP World Unit 7 (Global Conflict, c. 1900–present) sources

Every text excerpt was copied from raw text downloaded with curl and saved in sources/raw/ (second scans in raw/alt/ or alongside), then machine-checked against the raw text with a throwaway script: every stretch between ellipses, brackets, or footnote marks had to occur in the raw text once both were reduced to letters only. The stretches that did not match exactly were read by hand; every one is an OCR slip, a running head or page number inside a sentence, or a column break in MacMurray's two-column page, and each correction is listed in its file. Every edition or translation used was published in 1930 or earlier, or is a work of the U.S. government (Roosevelt, Wilson, Ayres, Jackson, the Hossbach translation, the Strategic Bombing Survey, Historical Statistics). Images are public-domain files on Wikimedia Commons, confirmed with the Commons API (raw/commons/*.json) and downloaded to raw/commons/ (a standard thumbnail size, because Wikimedia refused full-size downloads with HTTP 429). Data come from U.S. government statistics.

Notes:
- Fall 2026 CED Unit 7 has nine topics (7.1–7.9) and Learning Objectives A–I; see ced_unit7_excerpt.txt.
- Not used because no edition published by 1930 (or U.S. government edition) could be opened here: Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles of the People (Price translation, 1927), the Plan of Ayala in an old translation, Stalin's 1928–1931 speeches, Cárdenas's 1938 speech, the King–Crane report. Sun Yat-sen's Memoirs of a Chinese Revolutionary (c. 1918/1927) was downloaded but not used: its English, apparently made from a Russian version, garbles names and places (e.g., "Columbia" for Colorado).
- The Wikimedia API and upload server answered most requests from this shell with HTTP 429; requests were spaced by two to three minutes and retried. Lucien Jonas's 1917 poster for the Journée de l'Armée d'Afrique et des Troupes Coloniales was not found on Commons as a public-domain file (only a CC BY-SA photograph of it), so the exam image is a German war-loan poster instead.
- Passages left out on purpose: Kemal's charges against Greek and Armenian organizations (P3), Morgenthau's disparaging lines about Turks and Bedouins (P16), Du Bois's quotation of racial slurs (X2), the most graphic lines of Owen's poem (P6).
