Document: The Atlantic Charter, joint declaration of President Franklin D. Roosevelt and Prime Minister Winston Churchill, 14 August 1941 (issued by the White House and 10 Downing Street). Official declaration; the U.S. text is a government work.
Text: Avalon Project, https://avalon.law.yale.edu/wwii/atlantic.asp -> raw/avalon_wwii_atlantic.html
CUTS (in order): Preamble and points 1–4 complete. Cut (…) after point 4: point 5 ("Fifth, they desire to bring about the fullest collaboration ... social security;"). Point 6 complete. Cut (…): point 7 ("Seventh, such a peace should enable all men to traverse the high seas and oceans without hindrance;"). Point 8: first two sentences complete; cut (…) the last sentence ("They will likewise aid and encourage all other practicable measure which will lighten for peace-loving peoples the crushing burden of armaments.") and the signatures.
CONTEXT: Meeting at Placentia Bay, Newfoundland, 9–12 August 1941, aboard USS Augusta and HMS Prince of Wales. Lend-Lease Act March 1941; German invasion of the USSR 22 June 1941; Pearl Harbor 7 Dec 1941. Churchill's House of Commons statement of 9 September 1941 said the third point applied primarily to the nations of Europe under the Nazi yoke and was "a quite separate problem" from the development of self-government in regions owing allegiance to the British Crown (India, Burma, and other parts of the Empire) (used in E22). Checked in Hansard, HC Deb 9 September 1941, vol. 374, cols. 68–69 (https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1941/sep/09/war-situation, read with WebFetch): "the Joint Declaration does not qualify in any way the various statements of policy which have been made from time to time about the development of constitutional government in India, Burma or other parts of the British Empire"; "At the Atlantic meeting, we had in mind, primarily, the restoration of the sovereignty, self-government and national life of the States and nations of Europe now under the Nazi yoke"; "quite a separate problem from the progressive evolution of self-governing institutions in the regions and peoples which owe allegiance to the British Crown."
Attribution (2026-11-08b): Churchill described as "prime minister of the United Kingdom" (the Charter itself says "His Majesty's Government in the United Kingdom"). Glossary "general security" shortened to "safety for all nations" so it does not point to E21's key.
