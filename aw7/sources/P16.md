Para 4: begins after a cut (…) of the comparison with earlier massacres; "Undoubtedly religious fanaticism was an impelling motive with the Turkish and Kurdish rabble who slew Armenians" cut (…): "as a service to Allah," then "but the men who really conceived the crime had no such motive." SENSITIVITY: "rabble" is Morgenthau's word (glossed neutrally); his generalizations about Turks and Bedouins are left out.
CONTEXT: deportation law of 27 May 1915 (Tehcir); Sarıkamış defeat Jan 1915; historians' estimates of Armenian deaths about 600,000–1.2 million. The book was written with Burton J. Hendrick and published while the U.S. was at war with Germany (though not with the Ottoman Empire).
