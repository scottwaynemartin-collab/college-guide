ADAPTATION IN THE BANK (every cut):
The caption and description were written from the image. The caption quotes and translates only the heading and the first words of the German text under the right-hand picture ('Hie sitzt der Antichrist im tempel gottes', 'Ablaß'), which can be read in the downloaded file; the translation is the bank's own. The caption names the pope by his triple crown; it does not name Leo X. On the left page, the object on the floor is the overturned money changers' table (the German text's 'die Zahlbrett umbkahrt'); coins and a money box are not clearly visible, so since version 2026-11-09b the caption says 'an overturned money table lies on the floor' and the long description says 'a money changers' table lies knocked over on the floor'.

CONTEXT NOTES:
Melanchthon wrote the Latin and German captions; the pamphlet appeared in May 1521, weeks after the Diet of Worms. Some scholars attribute the woodcuts to Cranach himself, others to his workshop; the attribution says 'Workshop of'.
