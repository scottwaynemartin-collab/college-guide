CONTEXT NOTES:
Swedish forces landed at Peenemünde in Pomerania in late June/early July 1630. The duchy of Prussia, held by the Hohenzollern elector of Brandenburg as a fief of the Polish crown, had been drawn into the Polish-Swedish war of 1626–1629; the footnote gives only that the elector ruled it under the king of Poland. France promised subsidies to Sweden in the Treaty of Bärwalde (January 1631).
