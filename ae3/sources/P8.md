NOTES ON WHAT IS SHOWN:
Only the four columns above are used. King's 'Holland' is the whole Dutch Republic: elsewhere in the same work he compares England with 'the 7 provinces of Holland' (Chalmers, p. 413) and gives 'Holland' 8 million acres and 2,200,000 people (p. 441), figures that fit the whole Republic.

CONTEXT NOTES:
King wrote in 1696, during the Nine Years' War (1688-1697). His figures are estimates; the note says so.
