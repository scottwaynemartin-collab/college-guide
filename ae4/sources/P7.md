woman  had  no  knowledge  either  of  the  arts  or  of

Hers  was  a  singular  character,  not  easily  discerned 
and  described,  because  the  colouring  was  faint  and  much 
was  thrown  into  shade;  it  was  rather  marked  too,  but 
had  none  of  those  leading  features  by  which  a  character 
is  denned  and  distinguished.  She  was  kind,  with  little 
sensibility;  beneficent,  without  any  of  the  charms  of 
benevolence ;  prompt  in  succouring  the  unfortunate,  but

uneasiness.  She  was  a  sure,  faithful  and  even  active 
friend ;  yet  she  served  her  friends  with  a  timid  anxiety, 
lest  her  own  credit  and  tranquility  should  be  endangered.
