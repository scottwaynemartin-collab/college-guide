CONTEXT NOTES:
- Commodore Perry's ships forced Japan to sign the Treaty of Kanagawa (1854); the 1858 treaties were unequal.
- The Tomioka Silk Mill opened in 1872 with French machinery and French instructors; it was sold to private owners (Mitsui) in 1893.
- Japan defeated China (1894–95) and Russia (1904–05), annexed Taiwan (1895) and Korea (1910).
