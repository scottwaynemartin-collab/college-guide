As to the regular and fixed assessment of the Taxes, it is 
very important to settle that matter, for the State which is forced 
to incur many expenses for the defence of its Territory cannot 
obtain the money necessary for its Armies and other services 
except by means of contributions levied on its subjects. Although, 
thanks be to God, our Empire has for some time past been de-

CONTEXT NOTES:
