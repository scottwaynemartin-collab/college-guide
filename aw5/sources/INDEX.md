# INDEX: AP World Unit 5 (Revolutions, c. 1750–1900) sources

Every text excerpt was copied from raw text downloaded with curl and saved in sources/raw/ (second scans in raw/alt/), then machine-checked piece by piece against the raw text with a throwaway script: every stretch between ellipses or brackets had to occur in the raw text after whitespace and line-end hyphens were normalized. The 23 stretches that did not match exactly were read by hand, and a fuzzy word match put all but two at 0.88 or higher; every mismatch is an OCR slip, a running head, a footnote that breaks a sentence across a page, or a marginal note, and each is listed in its file. Every edition or translation used was published in 1930 or earlier. Images are public-domain files on Wikimedia Commons, confirmed with the Commons API and downloaded to raw/commons/. Data come from published scholarship.
