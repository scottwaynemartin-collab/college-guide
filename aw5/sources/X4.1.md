ADAPTATION IN THE BANK:
- Two cuts: the sentence about the children piecing broken ends, and the closing argument that children thrive better in factories than at home.

CONTEXT NOTES:
- Ure wrote after the 1833 Factory Act and during the Ten Hours campaign. He defended the factory system and its owners and is a classic example of a pro-industry point of view.
- Engels and later Marx attacked Ure by name as the apologist of the factory system.
