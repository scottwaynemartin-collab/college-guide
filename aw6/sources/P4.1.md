The repeated calls for killing are left out on purpose.

CONTEXT NOTES:
The Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act (1856) permitted, but did not require, remarriage; the footnote says so. Nagpur was annexed under the 'doctrine of lapse' (1853–1854); Awadh (capital Lucknow) was annexed in 1856 for 'misrule'.
