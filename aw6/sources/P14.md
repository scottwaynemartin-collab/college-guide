ADAPTATION IN THE BANK (every cut):
Only the twelve periods from 1841 to 1900 are charted. The chart note repeats the compilers' warning that the totals 'furnish only a rough picture'.

CONTEXT NOTES:
The compilers explain the dips: bad harvests, the 1857 crisis, and an anti-immigrant party in the 1850s; the Civil War; the panic of 1873; and the Argentine crisis of 1890.
