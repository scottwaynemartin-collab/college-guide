CONTEXT NOTES:
Rhodes (1853–1902) was prime minister of the Cape Colony (1890–1896) and head of the British South Africa Company (royal charter 1889), which conquered the Ndebele kingdom in 1893 and suppressed the Ndebele and Shona risings of 1896–1897.
