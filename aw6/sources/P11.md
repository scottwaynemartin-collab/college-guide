Article VI. 

The Government of Her Britannic Majesty having 
been obliged to send out an Expedition to demand and 
obtain redress for the violent and unjust Proceedings 
of the Chinese High Authorities towards Her Britannic 
Majesty's Officer and Subjects, the Emperor of China 
agrees to pay the sum of Twelve Millions of Dollars on 
account of the Expenses incurred, and Her Britannic 
Majesty's Plenipotentiary voluntarily agrees, on be- 
half of Her Majesty, to deduct from the said amount 
of Twelve Millions of Dollars, any sums which may 
have been received by Her Majesty's combined Forces 
as Ransom for Cities and Towns in China, subsequent 
to the 1st day of August 1841.

OCR / TEXT NOTES:
The Customs edition prints English and Chinese in parallel columns, so the OCR interleaves Chinese characters; the English wording was read around them and matches Mayers (1906) word for word apart from capitalization ('Hong-merchants (or Co-hong)' in Mayers).

ADAPTATION IN THE BANK (every cut):
Articles II, III, V, and X are quoted. Cuts: end of Article II ('to be the medium of communication … duly discharged'); end of Article V (the $3 million for the Hong merchants' debts); end of Article X (transit duties; the printed text leaves the rate blank). Article IV (the $6 million for opium) is omitted so that P31 tests outside knowledge.

CONTEXT NOTES:
The Supplementary Treaty of the Bogue (1843) added extraterritoriality and most-favored-nation treatment.
