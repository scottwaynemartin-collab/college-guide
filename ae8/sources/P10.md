CONTEXT NOTES:
War (1914–1917), revolution (1917) and civil war (1918–1921) with War Communism cut output per person to about 37 percent of the 1913 level. NEP (1921–1928) restored it; the first Five-Year Plan began in 1928 and collectivization in 1929. The famine of 1932–1933 killed millions, especially in Ukraine and Kazakhstan.
