CONTEXT NOTES:
The U.S. stock market crashed in October 1929; American lending to Germany had already slowed in 1928–1929. Britain left the gold standard in September 1931; France stayed on gold until 1936. Hitler became chancellor in January 1933; German rearmament and public works drove recovery.
