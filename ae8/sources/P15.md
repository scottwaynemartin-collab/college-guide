CONTEXT NOTES:
France asked for an armistice on 17 June 1940 and signed it on 22 June. Hitler issued this order on 16 July 1940. The Battle of Britain ran from July to October 1940; the Luftwaffe failed to destroy the RAF, and on 17 September 1940 Hitler postponed Operation Sea Lion indefinitely. Planning for the invasion of the Soviet Union began that summer (Directive No. 21, Barbarossa, 18 December 1940).
