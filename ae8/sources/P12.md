CONTEXT NOTES:
The laws were drafted in a few days for the party rally. Article 5 of the First Regulation defined a Jew mainly by grandparents: anyone with three or four Jewish grandparents counted as a Jew whatever his or her own religion, and a person with two Jewish grandparents counted as a Jew if, among other cases, he or she belonged to the Jewish religious community (Art. 5(2)(a); NCA's "two full Jewish parents" renders the German "Großelternteilen," grandparents). A grandparent counted as "full-blooded" if he or she had belonged to the Jewish religious community (Art. 2(2), second sentence, applied by Art. 5(1); raw lines 585–587, 624–632). The bank's notes (E8-P34 why-not, E8-P35 explanation) were reworded in v2026-11-09b so they do not say a person's own religion never mattered. Kristallnacht followed on 9–10 November 1938.
