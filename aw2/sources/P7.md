EXCERPT AS USED (… = cut; [ ] = added clarification):

[GUJARAT.] … The native products comprise great quantities of indigo, red kino, myrobolans and foreign cotton stuffs of every colour. Every year these goods are transported to the Ta-shi* countries for sale.

[THE ZANZIBAR COAST.] … The inhabitants are of Ta-shi stock and follow the Ta-shi religion. They wrap themselves in blue foreign cotton stuffs and wear red leather shoes. … The products of the country consist of elephants' tusks, native gold, ambergris and yellow sandal-wood. Every year Hu-ch'a-la [Gujarat] and the Ta-shi localities along the sea-coast send ships to this country with white cotton cloth, porcelain, copper, and red cotton to trade.

[THE BERBERA COAST.] The country of Pi-p'a-lo contains four cities … The land produces many camels and sheep, and the people feed themselves with the flesh and milk of camels and with baked cakes. The (other) products are ambergris, big elephants' tusks and big rhinoceros horns. … The land is also rich in putchuck, liquid storax gum, myrrh, and tortoise shell of extraordinary thickness, for which there is a great demand in other countries.
