EXCERPT AS USED (… = cut; [ ] = added clarification):

… In the fifth year of Yung-lo (A.D. 1407) the eunuch Cheng Ho [Zheng He] and others conveyed to the king letters patent conferring on him (the title of vassal king) and different gifts to his highest officers …

The king [the Zamorin] reveres the Buddha and shows respect to elephants and cows. … The king is a Nan-p'i [Nair]*. He does not eat beef. His highest officers are Moslims, they do not eat pork. Formerly the king made a pact with the Moslims, mutually forbidding to eat (beef and pork). …

All the affairs of the country are conducted by two high officers who are, at present, Musulmans. … There are some twenty to thirty mosques. …

In their usages they like sincerity. All the merchandize brought here by Chinese junks is under the control of the two high officers who send brokers to come to an understanding as to prices, which are not subsequently changed. And their mode of counting is to rely solely on the bending of the fingers, and they do not make an error of a fraction. …
