1. **E5-E08 (X3 Goya): the key and explanation get the order of events wrong.** The executions shown happened on May 3, 1808. Napoleon named Joseph king on June 6, 1808 (sources/P11.md says June too). So "resistance after Napoleon made his brother Joseph king" is false for this event.
   - Choice B → `Spanish resistance to French troops as Napoleon seized Spain's throne`
   - Explanation → `In 1808 French troops occupied Spain, and Napoleon forced Spain's royal family to give up the throne. Madrid rose up against the French on May 2, and French troops shot captured rebels the next day. In June Napoleon made his brother Joseph king. Goya painted the scene in 1814.`

   - Update the X6.md note to match. The E17 rationale still works.
