CONTEXT NOTES:
The line replaced the one in Alexander VI's bull Inter caetera (1493), 100 leagues west of the Azores and Cape Verde Islands. In the bank the treaty is paired with Grotius (X2.2): one divides the oceans between two crowns, the other claims free trade for all nations.
