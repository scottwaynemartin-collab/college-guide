Among the princes of our time Ferdinand of Aragon, the 
present king of Spain, may almost be accounted a new

ADAPTATION IN THE BANK (every cut):
Paragraphs 1–3 are complete (the opening of ch. XXI as Robinson prints it). Paragraph 4 is cut after 'and finally attacked France' (the sentence continues '; and being thus constantly busied in planning and executing vast designs, he ...'). The footnote on 'the Moors' is editorial: Machiavelli's Italian word is 'Marrani', which Thomson renders 'Moors'; the footnote gives the expulsions of 1492 (Jews) and 1502 (Muslims of Castile).

CONTEXT NOTES:
Machiavelli wrote while Ferdinand (d. 1516) was still 'the present king of Spain'. Used for the new monarchs (topic 1.5) and Machiavelli's view of rulers' use of religion (topic 1.2).
