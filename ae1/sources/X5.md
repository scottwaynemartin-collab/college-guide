CONTEXT NOTES:
Coleman (p. 4) dates the treatise to 1440, 'when he was secretary to Alfonso, king of Aragon, Sicily, and Naples', and calls it 'part of the campaign which that king was conducting against Pope Eugenius IV in furtherance of his claims to Italian territories' (the basis of E1-E14). Coleman's footnote 3 on p. 85 adds that 'the word satrap was in fact applied to higher officials at Rome only in the middle of the eighth century', which supports Valla's argument that the document is a later forgery (E1-E13 tests his philological method).
