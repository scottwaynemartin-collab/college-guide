CONTEXT NOTES:
Burckhardt's thesis that Renaissance Italy produced modern individualism, and that the cause was 'the political circumstances of Italy', is the classic interpretation that later historians (for example C. H. Haskins on the 'twelfth-century renaissance') qualified. E1-E02 asks what would qualify it; E1-E01 asks for the cause Burckhardt names in the text.
