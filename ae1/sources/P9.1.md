ADAPTATION IN THE BANK (every cut):
The epilogue is complete from 'Thus end I this book' to 'finished in one day.' except one cut after 'For all the books of this story': 'named the "Recuyell of the Historyes of Troye,"' (Knight's single quotation marks). The text continues 'Which book I presented to my said redoubted lady ...' (cut). The bank adds no brackets; '[expense]' is Knight's gloss on 'dispense'.

CONTEXT NOTES:
Caxton learned printing in Cologne in 1471–1472 and set up the first press in England at Westminster in 1476. Paired with P9.2 for printing and vernacular languages (E1-P25 to E1-P27).
