CONTEXT NOTES:
Grotius wrote the text in 1604–1605 as chapter XII of his brief De Jure Praedae, defending the Dutch East India Company's capture of the Portuguese carrack Santa Catarina (1603); it was published separately in 1609. Portugal was then ruled by the Spanish king (1580–1640).
