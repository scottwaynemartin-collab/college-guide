CONTEXT NOTES:
Las Casas wrote that he did not know whether to laugh or weep at the Requirement (E1-E21 keys Dominican friars such as Las Casas as its strongest critics). Queen Juana ('Doña Juana') became queen of Castile in 1504; her father Ferdinand governed Castile for her from 1507 until his death in 1516.
