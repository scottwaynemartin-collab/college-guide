CONTEXT NOTES:
The islanders are the Taíno (Lucayans) of Guanahaní (San Salvador), in the Bahamas; the attribution now says 'the first island he reached, in the Bahamas' (earlier 'in the Caribbean', a common loose usage). Credit line (2026-11-09b): the last sentence now reads 'The journal survives only in a shortened copy made by Bartolomé de Las Casas.' Markham's introduction (pp. v–vi, raw lines 455–525) says the journal 'is now lost', that Las Casas 'gives a very full abstract', and that apart from the prologue 'The rest is an abstract of the entries of each day, but there are long and frequent quotations, word for word'; Markham translates Navarrete's printing of that abstract. 'Copy' alone undersold the gap. Paired with Montaigne (P12.2) to contrast a conqueror's view with a critic of European cruelty (E1-P34).
