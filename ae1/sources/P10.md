CONTEXT NOTES:
The map is the first known to use the name 'America'; the accompanying book, Cosmographiae introductio (1507), explains the name. The Library of Congress bought the only surviving copy in 2003. Used for printing and the spread of geographic knowledge (E1-P28 to E1-P30).
