CONTEXT NOTES:
The act confirming the Star Chamber's powers was passed in 1487 (3 Henry VII). Bacon wrote in 1621, after his fall from office, more than a century after the events, and calls the Oxford story a 'report' (E1-E17). Paired in the unit with Lea on the Santa Hermandad (P16) for the new monarchs' control of the nobles (E1-E18).
