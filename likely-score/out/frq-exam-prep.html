How to write it

T1-
Topic sentenceSay the prompt back in your own words and give your reason in general words. No names, dates, or numbers yet — save those for E2-.
E2-
EvidenceThe proof. This is where the name, date, number, or place lives.
A3-
AnalysisWhy the proof makes your reason true. Start with "The reason this matters is that…"

Identify and Define parts skip TEA — those get one clean line. In our classroom approach, Describe, Explain, and Compare practice all three: T1-, E2-, A3-. Our classroom organization tip: the specific lives in E2-. If you already used it in T1-, you spent it too early.
