New to AP?

First AP class. Here's the whole game.

Most of you are ninth graders in your first college-level course. Nobody expects you to know how it works yet. Now you do.

01

The exam is two halves

60 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, then 3 free-response questions (FRQs) in 75 minutes. Each half is 50% of your score. You get a 1–5; a 3 is passing and most colleges give credit for it.

02

Vocabulary is the whole first half

Almost every multiple-choice question is a vocab word in disguise. If you know the words in Levels 1 and 2, you can answer questions you've never seen. That's why this site exists.

03

FRQs are seven small questions, not an essay

Each FRQ has parts A–G worth one point each. The first word (Identify, Define, Describe, Explain, Compare) tells you exactly how much to write. Level 3 teaches you that language.

04

Studying is reps, not rereading

Flip a card and decide. Play a round and see what you missed. Write an answer and check it. Fifteen minutes of that beats an hour of highlighting — which is what the three-night plan below is built on.

If you have 10 minutes

Readiness Check, then flip the cards for the one topic it flagged.

If you have 30 minutes

Flip Cards until you clean-sweep the "not mastered" filter, then one teacher challenge.

If you have an hour

Readiness Check → Flip Cards on the misses → two games → four FRQ reps in the FRQ Lab.

How to study

The three-night plan.

This is the routine. It works because each night uses the level above it.

Night 1

Clean-sweep the Flip Cards

Run the whole deck. Anything in Still learning gets re-run until the round ends with zero. If you can't clean-sweep the cards, you are not ready for the test — that's the bar.

Night 2

Beat a teacher in the Arcade

Take the Readiness Check, then play until you beat at least one teacher challenge. Your "focus words" list on the Arcade page tells you what to drill next.

Night 3

Do the reps in the FRQ Lab

Read the Verb Playbook once. Then do the reps for the topics the Readiness Check flagged — type your answer, reveal the model, score yourself. Finish with the Boss FRQ.
