Caption shown to students: The emperor Jahangir sits on an hourglass-shaped throne in front of a huge golden halo shaped like the sun, with a crescent moon behind it. He hands a book to an elderly Sufi shaikh with a long white beard. Below the shaikh stand a man in a turban whom scholars identify as an Ottoman sultan, King James I of England (copied from an English portrait), and the artist Bichitr, who holds up a small painting. Winged children fly overhead, and two more sit at the base of the hourglass, where one writes on it. Lines of Persian poetry fill the panels above and below.
