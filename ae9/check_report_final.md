1. **E9-E06: only the key mentions India or Pakistan.** The stem asks about "the ties between Britain and the new Dominions" (India and Pakistan). All three distractors are about European migrants, so a student can drop them on topic alone. Replace them with Britain/South Asia developments that the ties did not cause.
   - A → `India's leading role in the Non-Aligned Movement`
   - B → `Britain's entry into the European Community in 1973`
   - C → `the wars between India and Pakistan over Kashmir`
   - Rationale A → `Incorrect. India stayed in the Commonwealth, but it refused to join either side in the Cold War. Non-alignment came from India's own foreign policy, not from its ties with Britain.`
   - Rationale B → `Incorrect. Joining the European Community pulled Britain's trade toward Europe and away from the Commonwealth. It did not grow out of Britain's ties with India and Pakistan.`
   - Rationale C → `Incorrect. The fighting over Kashmir began in 1947. It grew out of partition and the dispute over which new country Kashmir would join, not out of ties with Britain.`
   - D (key) and its rationale are unchanged.

2. **E9-P33: all three distractors fall to the passage alone.** B, C and D each contradict "economic expansion is not an end in itself", so the item never tests what the 1968 protesters wanted.
