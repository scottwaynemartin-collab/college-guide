EXCERPT AS PRINTED IN THE BANK:

> The Revolutionary Workers and Peasants Government of Hungary declares that Imre Nagy's requests to the United Nations to have the Hungarian question discussed in the United Nations have no legal force and cannot be considered as requests emanating from Hungary as a State. The Revolutionary Workers and Peasants Government objects categorically to any discussion of the said question either by the Security Council or by the General Assembly because that question is within the exclusive jurisdiction of the Hungarian People's Republic.

NOTES ON MISMATCHES, CUTS AND CORRECTIONS:
The cable is typed in capitals with 'STOP' between sentences. In the bank it is set in ordinary capitals with periods for STOP (disclosed in the credit line); the signature lines are dropped. Because of the case change and STOP, the machine check reports a mismatch for this stretch; the words are otherwise identical (read by hand).
