How to write it · the Panther Creek DBQ

Context
2 + 2 sentencesBefore the time period, then during it. Real history, named.
Thesis
1 sentenceRestate the question + name your two categories.
Body
3 docs eachTopic sentence → describe (never quote) each document in your own words, tag it (Doc N), say what it proves. Six described across the essay; four used to support. One (EBD) — evidence beyond the documents.
Sourcing
2 documentsIntroduce the author → connect to your argument → describe and tie back → state the purpose outright. Never “Document 3.”
Link
3+ sentencesThe 7th point: six documents described and four used AND a paragraph connecting your two categories to each other.
Labels
Always(Doc N), (EBD), and the sourcing blocks are how a reader finds your points in ten seconds.

Rubric: Thesis 1 · Context 1 · Evidence from the documents 2 · Evidence beyond the documents 1 · Sourcing 1 · Complexity 1 = 7. The complexity point is earned in this class by six documents described, four used, plus a linking paragraph.
