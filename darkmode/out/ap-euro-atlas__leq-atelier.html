How to write it · the Panther Creek LEQ

Context
4 sentencesTwo about the world before the time period, two about what was happening during it. Real history, named.
Thesis
1 sentenceRestate the question + name your two categories. Each category becomes a body paragraph.
Body
8+ sentences eachTopic sentence naming the category → evidence labeled (EV1) + explain it → (EV2) + explain it → 2–3 reasoning sentences (because → so).
Link
3+ sentencesThe 6th point: all four EVs explained AND a paragraph connecting your two categories to each other.
Labels
AlwaysLabel everything. A reader finds every point in ten seconds. Unlabeled essays top out at a 90.

Rubric: Thesis 1 · Context 1 · Evidence 2 · Analysis & Reasoning 2 = 6. The second reasoning point is earned in this class by four explained EVs plus a linking paragraph — not by four EVs alone.
