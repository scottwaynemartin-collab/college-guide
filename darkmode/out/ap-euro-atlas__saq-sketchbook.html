How to write it · Spill the TEA

T1-
Topic sentenceSay the prompt back in your own words and give your reason in general words. No names, dates, or treaties yet — save those for E2-.
E2-
EvidenceThe proof. This is where the name, the date, the treaty, the war, or the place lives.
A3-
AnalysisWhy the proof makes your reason true. Start with “The reason this matters is that…”

Identify parts skip TEA — those get one clean line. Every Describe, Explain, and Compare part gets all three: T1-, E2-, A3-. The rule that fixes most flow problems: the specific lives in E2-. If you already used it in T1-, you spent it too early.
